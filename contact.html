What you can write about

  • Feedback and suggestions — ideas for new companies or sources to follow, features, or anything that feels off.
  • Corrections — a post in the wrong category, a broken link, or a source that is out of date.
  • Removal requests — see the note below if you are a rights holder.
  • Press and partnerships — collaborations, listings or anything commercial.

Source and removal requests

AI Radar is an aggregator: it reads publicly available feeds and links each item back to the original article. All rights to the posts remain with their original sources, as set out in the Disclaimer. If you are a rights holder and would like a reference to your content removed, email info@ai-radar.eu with the link in question and I will act on reasonable requests promptly.

Privacy

Emailing me means I receive your email address and your message, used only to reply to you. AI Radar has no contact form and stores nothing on its side. How the dashboard itself handles data is described in the Privacy Statement.
