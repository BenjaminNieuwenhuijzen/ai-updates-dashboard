About Model Monitor

Model Monitor shows updates from seven AI companies. On the live site it reads one combined feed that is rebuilt every few hours on the server, so it loads fast and reliably; opened as a local file it fetches each company’s feed directly. OpenAI and Google publish an official feed; for the other companies a community feed is used, which may lag behind. Click any post to open the original article. A blue dot marks posts that arrived since the last refresh. Every post is automatically assigned a category (Model update, Product, Research, Company, Safety or Other) based on its title and summary; use the category bar and the search box at the top to filter. This classification is an automated estimate and not foolproof.
