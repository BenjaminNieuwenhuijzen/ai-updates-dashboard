About AI Radar

A personal, non-commercial project by Benjamin Nieuwenhuijzen.

AI Radar is a live dashboard that collects the latest news and updates from the leading AI companies on one page. Every post links straight to the original article. AI Radar publishes nothing of its own.

What the dashboard shows

Each company has its own card, from OpenAI and Anthropic to Mistral and DeepSeek. The Across AI card adds industry news that is not tied to a single company. You can filter by category or search, follow a subset of companies, drag the cards into your own order, save posts for later, or switch to a timeline that lists everything chronologically. The footer of the dashboard shows the current number of companies and the split between official and community feeds; that text is generated from the live configuration, so it is always up to date.

Where the posts come from

AI Radar reads publicly available RSS and Atom feeds. Each card states its source: an official feed is maintained by the company itself, a community feed is maintained by others and may lag behind. A scheduled job fetches all feeds every two hours and rebuilds the page data. Nothing is rewritten or shortened by hand; posts appear as their source published them.

What is automated, and therefore fallible

Three things on the page are produced by software rather than a person, and each one can be wrong:

  • Categories (Research, Product, Developer and so on) are assigned from a post's title and summary. A mislabelled post is an estimate gone wrong, not an editorial judgement.
  • Card order follows recent news activity, unless you drag the cards yourself.
  • The "Today in AI" briefing at the top is written by an AI model from the most recent headlines.

For anything that matters, click through and read the original article.

Who makes it

AI Radar is made and maintained by Benjamin Nieuwenhuijzen. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any of the companies it follows. All rights to the posts remain with their original sources, as set out in the Disclaimer. The dashboard needs no account and collects no personal data about you; the details are in the Privacy Statement.

Suggestions for new sources, corrections and other feedback are welcome via the contact page. Prefer your own reader? There is an RSS feed with everything combined.
